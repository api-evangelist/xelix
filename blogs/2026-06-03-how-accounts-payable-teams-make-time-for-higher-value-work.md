At every P2P conference we attend, we always hear how stretched AP and shared services teams are. Query volumes up, headcount flat, and somewhere in the background, a leadership message to "be more strategic." It's well-meaning but it's also hard to square with an AP helpdesk still processing the same types of vendor queries it was processing two years ago. At the Procure to Pay network event, hosted by The Shared Services Forum UK, we ran a live benchmarking session with 40 shared services and P2P leaders to get closer to the real question: are AP teams busy with the right work?
