Xelix Connect brought together more than 50 senior leaders from across AP, Finance, Shared Services and P2P for a day of sessions, panel discussions, product updates and peer conversation. As with every Xelix Connect event, the goal wasn't to deliver a series of presentations and send everyone home with a notebook full of slides. It was to create space for practitioners to learn from each other, compare experiences and have the kinds of conversations that don't always happen in day-to-day work.
