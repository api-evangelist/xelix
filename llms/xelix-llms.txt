# Xelix

> Xelix is an AI-powered Accounts Payable control platform (London, UK + Wilmington, DE) that bolts onto existing ERP, P2P, and email systems to prevent duplicate/incorrect payments and fraud, reconcile supplier statements, resolve vendor queries with generative AI, cleanse master vendor data, and power AP reporting. 115M+ invoices processed annually for 200+ global customers; backed by Insight Partners ($160M, July 2025). ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certified, GDPR compliant, AWS-hosted (EU/US/UK data residency). Integration is file-based (daily SFTP transfer of invoice, workflow, and master vendor data) plus optional SSO and email integrations — Xelix publishes no public developer portal or documented API.
